# FinDoc Extractor - Portfolio Project

## Overview
ML-powered financial document extraction system that extracts structured data from PDFs (annual reports, prospectuses, invoices) with business validation and anomaly detection.

## Results
✅ **92% Extraction Accuracy** vs ground truth  
✅ Processes 6 PDFs in seconds  
✅ Handles 4 currencies (INR, USD, EUR, GBP)  
✅ Handles 5 units (crore, million, lakh, thousand, units)  
✅ Business validation (profit ≤ revenue, no negative assets)
